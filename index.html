Start here

  1. In Shopify admin, open Online Store → Themes and customize Trestle.
  2. Upload a logo and favicon under Theme settings → Brand. Check both desktop and mobile headers.
  3. Choose heading and body fonts under Typography; adjust the page width, margins, and colors in their theme settings.
  4. Create a main menu and footer menu under Content → Menus, then assign them in the Header and Footer sections.
  5. Add your products, collections, pages, policies, shipping rates, and payment methods in Shopify admin. Prices, availability, and checkout options come from Shopify.
  6. Replace demo text and images. Preview product, collection, cart, search, contact, and policy pages before publishing.

Products and inventory

Create products and variants in Shopify admin. Add option names such as Color and Size when shoppers need a choice. Track inventory on variants to display stock status. Check Shopify's selling settings if you want purchases to stop when inventory reaches zero. Set a compare-at price above the sale price to show a discount.

Delivery estimates

The cart displays an estimate only when you provide one. For product-specific estimates, create a single line text metafield with namespace and key custom.delivery_estimate on products or variants. A variant value takes priority. The Cart section also has a Default delivery estimate for a range that applies across your store. Only publish a range you can meet; destination-specific checkout dates depend on your shipping setup.

Product reviews

The Product reviews section supports an app block. Install a review app in your store, add its review widget block to the product template, and enable any required app embed. That app handles submissions, moderation, and ratings. Trestle does not collect reviews itself. Check the app's pricing and permissions before installing it.

Newsletter and first-order offer

The home page Newsletter section and Newsletter popup can advertise an offer. First create and activate a code in Shopify admin → Discounts. Enter that same code in each section's Discount code setting, and state its actual terms in First-order offer. The theme presents the code after signup and links to apply it. Shopify checks eligibility at checkout; a theme setting alone does not create a discount.

Use Enable popup, Show after scrolling, and Popup image to control its presentation. Replace example copy with your own.

Contact and policies

Create a Contact page with the contact page template. Enter a business email, phone, address, and support hours only when they are real for your business. The storefront contact form uses Shopify's contact form. Add your policy text under Settings → Policies and check every footer policy link.

Social media

Add your profile URLs under Theme settings → Social media. Leave unused URLs blank so their icons do not appear. The Footer section can override these links.

Common questions

Why is a product marked sold out?

Shopify controls availability from the selected variant's inventory and selling settings. Check that variant's inventory in admin.

Why does the first-order code not work?

Confirm that the code exists in Shopify Discounts, is active, and permits this customer, product, date, and order. Matching text in the theme editor is not enough.

Why are reviews missing?

Install and configure a compatible review app, add its widget block to the product template, and enable its app embed if required.

Why is a delivery estimate missing?

Add custom.delivery_estimate to the product or variant, or set the default in the Cart section. Leave it blank when one range cannot be promised across destinations.

Why are my photos different from the demo?

Demo product images belong to the demo store. Add your own product media in Shopify admin and select it in the theme editor.
